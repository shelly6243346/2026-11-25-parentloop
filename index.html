The problem

Care slips in the gap between siblings.

The doctor's visit is the easy part. The hard part is the week after, when rides, refills, and uncovered afternoons live in a scrolling chat.

The thread is the chart.

Dose changes, insurance numbers, and “who has Thursday” disappear under the next message.

One sibling holds the map.

The adult child who lives closest becomes the unpaid coordinator, and the others cannot see the load.

Gaps show up late.

A missed refill or a day with nobody scheduled becomes an emergency instead of a Tuesday task.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Is this a medical record?

No. Grandma Wang's profile is fiction, labeled sample data. Do not put a real person's care in this demo. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Does the note tool send a doctor's note to an AI?

No. The extractor uses rules written for this demo. It runs in your browser, it does not call a model, and it does not need an API key. The screen says so.

Are the prices real?

No. Free, Family at $12 a month, and Family Plus at $29 a month are hypothetical labels so the page looks like a product. Nothing can be purchased.

Who is the demo family?

Grandma Wang, 78, and siblings Amy, Ben, and Chloe. Switch the demo identity inside the board. My tasks follows the sibling you pick.

Can more than one sibling use it?

In this prototype, everyone shares the same browser storage on that device. A shipped product would sync a family. The demo lets you act as Amy, Ben, or Chloe so the handoff is visible.

What happens to a waitlist email?

It is saved in this browser only. ParentLoop does not send it anywhere. You can remove it under the form.
